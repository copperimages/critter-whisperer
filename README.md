# Critter Whisperer

A calm, phone-friendly way to hear the frogs, toads, crickets, cicadas, and katydids of Alabama. Pick a group, play a sound, and open an animal for a short note and the months it shows up.

The site is static. Species and photos are listed in `data/species.json`, the Alabama checklist is in `data/states.json`, recordings are in `sounds/`, and species photos are in `photos/`.

Live site: https://copperimages.github.io/critter-whisperer/

## Credits

Sounds and photos come from [iNaturalist](https://www.inaturalist.org) contributors and are used under the Creative Commons license each person chose. `data/species.json` keeps that attribution on every item: the recordist or photographer, the license, the date and place when we have them, and a link back to the observation or photo.

Many recordings are licensed for non-commercial use only (CC BY-NC). Others are CC BY or CC0. Some older photos are CC BY-NC-SA or CC BY-NC-ND. Please follow the license on each recording and photo.

Local recordings were made again from the original iNaturalist files. There is no noise reduction and no EQ that cuts high frequencies. Each editable file has a short fade at each end and is level-matched. After encoding, the call’s frequency band still holds at least most of the energy it had in the original. No clip had to be reverted to an unprocessed copy. No clip was time-trimmed: the one recording where people were talking also had the call running underneath that speech, so it was replaced instead of cut.

Five CC BY-NC-ND recordings still have a usable call and cannot be edited, so they stay as the original iNaturalist files: Southern Leopard Frog (John Trent), Bird-voiced Treefrog (Roger Birkhead), Pickerel Frog (John Trent), Japanese Burrowing Cricket (John Trent), and Broad-winged Tree Cricket (John Trent).

Fowler’s toad recording 3 is a CC BY-NC Atlanta recording by robotpie (observation 212791094). The Alabama file it replaced was nearly silent. This copy is level-matched only.
